# Fig POS — Feature Overview

Fig POS is a complete point-of-sale system built specifically for Pakistani mobile phone shops. It buys and sells new and used phones, tracks every device by IMEI, manages credit and customer balances, and turns every transaction into a clear picture of your business.

Everything below is available today.

---

## 1. Fast Selling at the Counter

- Ring up a sale in seconds — search by name, SKU, or IMEI and tap to add.
- Pick the exact physical phone by IMEI — choose which unit you're selling (new or used).
- Sell accessories (covers, chargers, earbuds) as simple quantity items.
- Quick walk-in customer creation right on the sale screen — the contact is saved and reused automatically for future sales.
- Split one bill across **cash, card, bank transfer, and credit** in any combination.
- Automatic change calculation — type the amount received, the change is worked out for you.
- Discounts on individual items or the whole order; price overrides controlled by permission.
- Print a clean, thermal-80mm friendly receipt showing IMEI, brand, and condition.
- Process sale returns / refunds — a returned phone goes back to stock as USED unless it's unopened.

## 2. Buying & Tracking Every Phone

- **Buy New** from vendors with bulk IMEI entry.
- **Buy Used** from walk-ins or vendors with a condition grade (A/B/C/D) and negotiated price.
- One physical device = one IMEI record. Every phone knows where it came from, from whom, when, and at what cost.
- **Device lookup**: type or paste any IMEI to see that exact phone's complete history.
- **PTA status** recorded (PTA / Non-PTA / SIM locked) so you always know what you're buying.
- Separate inventory views and stock counts for new vs used phones, each with its own valuation.
- Stock movements logged for every change — in, out, adjustment, transfer.
- Low-stock alerts per model.
- **Purchase orders** — record an order now, receive it later, and support partial deliveries.
- **Purchase returns** — send a faulty unit back and refund.

## 3. Credit, Collections & Balances

- Sell on credit and track each customer's running balance and limit.
- Partial credit — a bill can be partly paid now and the rest added to the customer's credit account (e.g. Rs 1,000 bill → Rs 500 now + Rs 500 on credit).
- Collections reduce the balance automatically, with a receipt for each collection.
- Overdue list showing days past due.
- One contact ledger showing everything bought from and sold to each person, plus their running balance.

## 4. Reservations & Consignment

- Reserve units for customers with optional deposits.
- Hold consignment stock and return it when needed.

## 5. Understand Your Business (Analytics & Reports)

- **Customizable dashboard** — add, remove, and rearrange KPI cards and charts; the layout is saved per user.
- See today's sales, net profit, units sold, stock on hand, stock value, returns, credit, expenses, and net cash at a glance.
- Charts: sales trend, payments by method, top products, new vs used sold, top sellers, phones vs accessories, PTA status, low stock.
- Reports for sales, purchases, profit (by brand/model/condition), expenses, and stock valuation.
- Payments summary grouped by cash, card, and each bank account.
- Receivables, payables, and credit-aging reports.
- **Z-report / end-of-day cash reconciliation** with expected closing and variance.
- Device lookup, ledger, sales/purchase lists, sale/purchase returns, and stock-movement reports.

## 6. Money & Bank

- Register your shop's bank accounts once (name, bank, account number, IBAN, holder).
- Accounts appear as tap-to-pay choices — the cashier never types an account number.
- Split one payment across two bank accounts.
- **Vouchers** (cash receiving / cash payment) — issue, edit, and reverse them with a full audit trail.
- **Expenses** — record shop expenses and see them against cash on the dashboard.

## 7. One Contact Record for Everyone

- A single record per person: **Walk-in / Customer / Vendor / Both** — upgradeable in place, never duplicated.
- A walk-in who sells you a used phone and buys a new one is one contact, with one ledger and one balance.
- Name, phone, email, address, notes, and credit limit.
- Deduplication warning when a phone number already exists.

## 8. Secure, Role-Based Access

- Simple login — username and 4-digit PIN only.
- Three roles: **Admin** (everything), **Manager** (sales, purchases, inventory, reports), **Cashier** (POS only — no cost/profit visibility).
- Fine-grained permissions: view costs, override price, refund, manage users, view money, and more.
- **Audit log** of every sale, purchase, void, refund, adjustment, and user change.

## 9. Professional Documents & Printing

- **Company profile** — your store name, address, phone, email, logo, receipt footer, currency, and tax rate.
- Print layouts for each document:
  - **Thermal 58/80mm receipt** — compact, for quick counter sales.
  - **A5/A4 invoice** — full letterhead + logo + itemized table.
  - **Purchase slip** — what you paid and the IMEI.
  - **Credit statement** — per-contact outstanding balance + payment history.
- Direct printing to thermal printers, or export to PDF.

## 10. Built for Your Shop

- Works on tablet, phone, or desktop — responsive and mobile-first.
- **Works offline** — the counter keeps working through a network drop, with a queue that syncs automatically when you're back online.
- Automatic backups with retention, plus manual backup and restore.
- A **Windows desktop app** that runs fully offline — no internet needed at the counter.
- Free online activation with a 10-digit code, plus automatic app updates.
- Global search across your whole business, and a wide range of colour themes to match your shop.

---

> **Made for the Pakistani market** — everything is priced in PKR (Rs), with trade-ins, used phones, credit, and cash handled the way a Pakistani phone shop actually works.
